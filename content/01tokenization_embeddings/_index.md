A model cannot read text. It can only do math on numbers. This chapter covers the two steps that
turn human language into something a neural network can learn from: **tokenization** (splitting text
into units and mapping them to integers) and **embeddings** (turning those integers into meaningful
vectors). You will also load the text corpus your model will be trained on.
